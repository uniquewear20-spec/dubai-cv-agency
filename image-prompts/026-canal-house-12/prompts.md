## Creative direction

A brick building in a glass city. The photography leans into texture: hand-laid brick glowing at golden hour, black steel window grids, reflections broken by kayaks on the canal. Interiors are lofts with high ceilings and big steel windows. Life on the promenade gives it energy: walkers, cyclists, a cafe on the ground floor.

## Visual identity

- **Look:** warm, textured urban photography: golden light on brick, reflections on the canal, black steel, life on the promenade, 35mm, photorealistic.
- **Palette:** burnt brick red, black steel, canal teal, oak and warm white.
- **Type and layout cues for the website:** industrial grotesque in heavy weights, unit numbers set large; brick red as the only strong colour.
