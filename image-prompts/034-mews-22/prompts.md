## Creative direction

A small house with a big address. The photography is quiet and precise, London-soft: wisteria, a glossy black door, wet cobbles after rain, then calm contemporary rooms inside old proportions. Small human moments (a dog walk, a delivery of flowers) make it feel like a real street.

## Visual identity

- **Look:** classic London property photography: soft overcast and early-morning light, glossy paint, wet cobbles, quiet elegance, 35mm, photorealistic.
- **Palette:** cream stucco, gloss black, London brick, wisteria lilac and brass.
- **Type and layout cues for the website:** classic book serif with small caps; lots of white; a deep black for the door colour as the accent.

## Consistency

Every prompt that shows the house repeats this description word for word, so Seedream draws the same one each time. Do not edit it:

> a two-storey London mews house of cream-painted stucco on a narrow cobbled lane, a glossy black front door with a brass knocker under a semicircular fanlight, black-framed sash windows with window boxes of white flowers, wisteria in full lilac bloom climbing the facade, a glossy black carriage door to the garage, neighbouring mews houses in soft pastel colours

- Generate 01 first; keep the house description word for word.
- Interiors share pale oak, plaster mouldings, linen, brass and soft London daylight.
- Every prompt ends with the same look and palette sentence, so the set matches.
- People are always clearly different individuals, often seen from behind or with faces out of frame, because Seedream repeats one face when people are not described apart. Never a real person, a brand or a logo.
- Grids (2x2) give four pictures from one generation; Claude cuts them into four files.
