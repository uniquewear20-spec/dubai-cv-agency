# 032 · QURUM CLIFF

**Category:** Real estate  
**Concept:** Six white villas stepped down a cliff above the Gulf of Oman in Muscat; a villa picker and an enquiry.  
**Website:** /showcase/qurum-cliff/  
**Images:** 10 generations, 19 images  
**Image status:** New art-directed prompts (27 Nov): generate these with Seedream 5.0 Pro

## Creative direction

White cubes on black rock above a deep blue sea, with the jagged mountains of Muscat behind. The photography is graphic and sculptural: hard light, crisp shadows, long views down the white stair to a private cove. Late sun turns the rock copper and the plaster gold. Everything is about descent toward the water.

## Visual identity

- **Look:** bold, graphic coastal architecture photography: hard sunlight, deep shadows, black rock, white plaster and a deep blue sea, copper light at sunset, 24-35mm, photorealistic.
- **Palette:** black basalt rock, plaster white, deep sea blue, date-palm green and copper.
- **Type and layout cues for the website:** heavy geometric sans capitals stacked like the villas; strong black and white layouts with one deep blue.

## Consistency

Every prompt that shows the villas repeats this description word for word, so Seedream draws the same one each time. Do not edit it:

> six white plaster villas stepped down a dark rocky cliff above the Gulf of Oman, each villa a cluster of cubic volumes with deep-set windows, shaded roof terraces under timber pergolas and a private pool cantilevered over the rocks, linked by a white stair winding down to a small cove with a wooden jetty, the jagged brown mountains of Muscat behind

- Generate 01 first; keep the villa description word for word.
- Interiors share white plaster, pale oak, linen and brass, always with the sea framed in a deep window.
- Every prompt ends with the same look and palette sentence, so the set matches.
- People are always clearly different individuals, often seen from behind or with faces out of frame, because Seedream repeats one face when people are not described apart. Never a real person, a brand or a logo.
- Grids (2x2) give four pictures from one generation; Claude cuts them into four files.
