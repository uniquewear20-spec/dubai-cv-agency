## Creative direction

KAI 8 is told from seat four. One long slab of pale hinoki, eight seats and a single chef; everything else falls away into warm darkness. Photography is intimate and still: pools of tungsten light on wood, steam, the gloss of rice and fish, hands at work, never a face. Close crops and slow reveals carry the site, so every picture leaves dark negative space for type.

## Visual identity

- **Look:** quiet, low-key Japanese counter photography, warm tungsten pin lights in deep shadow, 50mm at seated eye level, shallow depth of field, photorealistic.
- **Palette:** pale hinoki wood, black lacquer, rice white, tuna red and muted bronze.
- **Type and layout cues for the website:** tall condensed serif headlines against black, small calm sans for menus; generous dark space around each dish.

## Consistency

Every prompt that shows the counter repeats this description word for word, so Seedream draws the same one each time. Do not edit it:

> an intimate eight-seat omakase counter made from one thick slab of pale hinoki wood with a softly rounded, polished front edge, eight low black lacquer stools, a back wall of dark charred cedar boards, one narrow shelf of hand-thrown ceramic tea pots, a warm tungsten pin light recessed above each seat, a white linen curtain hanging at the entrance

- Dishes are always on matte black or dark grey hand-thrown ceramics on the hinoki, lit by one warm light from the left.

- People are always clearly different individuals, often seen from behind or with faces out of frame, because Seedream repeats one face when people are not described apart. Never a real person, a brand or a logo.
