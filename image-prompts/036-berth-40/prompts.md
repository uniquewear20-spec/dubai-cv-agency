## Creative direction

Live where your yacht sleeps. The building is shaped like stacked decks, and the water is part of every picture: hulls below balconies, teak underfoot, chrome catching sunlight. Mornings are crisp and sparkling; nights are mirrored lights on the marina. The site moves from the water up into the home and back down to the berth.

## Visual identity

- **Look:** glossy nautical architecture photography: white hulls, teak and chrome, sparkling navy water, crisp morning sun and glowing marina nights, 24-50mm, photorealistic.
- **Palette:** hull white, navy, teak, polished chrome and sea-glass green.
- **Type and layout cues for the website:** nautical condensed sans with numbered berths as a graphic system; navy and white with brass rules.
