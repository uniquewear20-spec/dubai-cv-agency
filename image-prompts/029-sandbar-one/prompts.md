# 029 · SANDBAR ONE

**Category:** Real estate  
**Concept:** A private island off Abu Dhabi with twelve beach villa plots and a marina; a plot picker, villa designs and an enquiry.  
**Website:** /showcase/sandbar-one/  
**Images:** 10 generations, 16 images  
**Image status:** New art-directed prompts (27 Nov): generate these with Seedream 5.0 Pro

## Creative direction

An island you arrive at by boat. Seen first from the air as a white sandbar in gradients of turquoise, then at water level where the shallows are so clear that boats seem to float on air. Architecture is low and white, shaded by palm-thatch pavilions, so the sea always dominates. Bright, clean, effortless luxury; dusk adds the romance.

## Visual identity

- **Look:** bright aerial and water-level island photography, saturated turquoise shallows, white sand, midday clarity and soft dusk, drone and 24mm, photorealistic.
- **Palette:** turquoise, white sand, palm green, sun-bleached teak and soft coral.
- **Type and layout cues for the website:** light, open sans capitals with plenty of tracking; layouts with big turquoise fields and very little chrome.

## Consistency

Every prompt that shows the island repeats this description word for word, so Seedream draws the same one each time. Do not edit it:

> a small low private island of white sand ringed by turquoise shallows, twelve low white villas with flat roofs and palm-thatch shade pavilions spaced along the beach, a crescent-shaped teak marina with a few white motor yachts, a central grove of date palms, a long sandbank trailing off the northern tip, in the Arabian Gulf off Abu Dhabi

- Generate 01 first; keep the island description word for word in every aerial and wide shot.
- Villa shots repeat the same villa: a low white flat-roofed villa with a palm-thatch pavilion and a pool that steps down into the sea.
- Every prompt ends with the same look and palette sentence, so the set matches.
- People are always clearly different individuals, often seen from behind or with faces out of frame, because Seedream repeats one face when people are not described apart. Never a real person, a brand or a logo.
- Grids (2x2) give four pictures from one generation; Claude cuts them into four files.
