# 024 · RIAD SEVEN FOUNTAINS

**Category:** Real estate  
**Concept:** Seven restored riads for sale behind one unmarked door in the Marrakech medina; the restoration story and a riad picker.  
**Website:** /showcase/riad-seven-fountains/  
**Images:** 11 generations, 26 images  
**Image status:** New art-directed prompts (27 Nov): generate these with Seedream 5.0 Pro

## Creative direction

Behind one plain door, seven courtyards. The story is a restoration: dust and scaffolding becoming tadelakt, zellige and running water. Photography looks down into courtyards and up to squares of sky, with light falling in shafts and the sound of water implied everywhere. Craftsmen's hands carry the restoration chapter; candlelit dinners carry the life.

## Visual identity

- **Look:** warm, shadowed courtyard photography in the Marrakech medina, light falling from the open sky into deep shade, saturated terracotta and zellige green, candlelight at night, 24-35mm, photorealistic.
- **Palette:** terracotta, zellige green, carved cedar brown, tadelakt cream and brass.
- **Type and layout cues for the website:** calligraphic-feeling serif headlines, geometric patterns as dividers taken from the zellige; warm cream backgrounds.
