# 022 · SAHRA STABLES ESTATE

**Category:** Real estate  
**Concept:** A private equestrian estate where the paddocks meet the dunes, on the desert edge outside Dubai: an estate tour, the stables and arena, and an enquiry.  
**Website:** /showcase/sahra-stables/  
**Images:** 11 generations, 17 images  
**Image status:** New art-directed prompts (27 Nov): generate these with Seedream 5.0 Pro

## Creative direction

An estate told like a country-house monograph moved to the desert. Long, low, earth-coloured architecture sits on the line where white paddock rails meet red dunes; Arabian horses give it life and scale. Light is always low and golden or blue at dawn, with dust in the air. The site moves from the arrival avenue to the house, the stables, the arena and out onto the dunes.

## Visual identity

- **Look:** warm country-estate photography at the desert's edge, low golden sun, fine dust glowing in the air, 35mm and 85mm, unhurried and photorealistic.
- **Palette:** sand, chestnut, olive green, bone white and saddle brown.
- **Type and layout cues for the website:** wide-tracked serif capitals for the estate name, a warm sans for text; long horizontal compositions to match the architecture.

## Consistency

Every prompt that shows the estate repeats this description word for word, so Seedream draws the same one each time. Do not edit it:

> a long, low single-storey estate house of rammed earth and sand-coloured lime render with a deep veranda roofed in dark timber on slender bronze columns, a flat roof with a thin bronze edge, tall oak louvred shutters, an inner courtyard of ghaf and olive trees, a matching rammed-earth stable block with tall arched openings and dark oak stable doors, white post-and-rail paddock fences, on the edge of red dunes outside Dubai

- Generate 01 first; keep the estate description exactly as written in every prompt that shows the buildings.
- The horses are always purebred Arabians: one dapple grey, one chestnut, one bay. Keep those colours when they reappear.
- Every prompt ends with the same look and palette sentence, so the set matches.
- People are always clearly different individuals, often seen from behind or with faces out of frame, because Seedream repeats one face when people are not described apart. Never a real person, a brand or a logo.
- Grids (2x2) give four pictures from one generation; Claude cuts them into four files.
