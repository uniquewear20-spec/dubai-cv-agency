How I workFrom a wall of maybes to one idea

The brief gets smaller. The idea gets bigger.

  1. Listen

    A day with the founders, the sales team and the people who pack the boxes. The brief is usually hiding there.

  2. One wall

    Everything goes on one wall, never in slides. If it does not survive a week on the wall, it goes.

  3. One idea

    If it takes a paragraph to explain, it is not one idea yet. We edit until it fits on a sticky note.

  4. Craft

    Type, light, casting and the edit. The idea is a promise; the craft is how we keep it.
