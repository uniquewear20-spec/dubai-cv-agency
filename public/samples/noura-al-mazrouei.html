    03 · ApproachHow we build

    Three rules, written on every site board.

    1. 01

      Build for the people who stay.

      Buyers visit once; residents live there for years. We design for maintenance, shade and the walk to the mosque, not for the brochure.

    2. 02

      Shade before glass.

      Deep balconies, screens and the right orientation first; air conditioning second. Comfort costs less when the building does the work.

    3. 03

      Finish what you start.

      We publish a schedule for every project and report against it every quarter, including when we are late.
