01 · Letter from the CFOAnnual Review 2026

Numbers that tell the truth, early.

Dear reader, over twenty-five years in finance I have learned that a good CFO is the first person to say what the numbers mean, not the last person to sign them.

At Lindqvist Industrial Group, a €2.3bn manufacturer with plants on three continents, my team rebuilt planning around rolling forecasts, released €180m of cost and moved the company to an investment-grade rating.

I work closely with boards and lenders, and I am at my best when a business is changing: new markets, a refinancing, a carve-out or a listing.

This review summarises the results my teams delivered, and the roles where I can help next.

Hannah BeckerChief Financial Officer
