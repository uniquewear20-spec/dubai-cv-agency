Dear colleagues, shareholders and friends,

In the spring of 2020 our order book fell by almost a fifth in ten weeks. It was the worst quarter of my working life, and at the time it felt like the only quarter that mattered.

It wasn't. Seen from here, that fall is a notch on a line that has roughly doubled in ten years. The decisions that made the difference were not the ones we took in the panic. They were the ones we had taken years before: to keep training apprentices through a downturn, to own the service of the pumps we sell, and to build our plants close to the customers who will need them for forty years.

A pump installed in a city's water network today will still be running in 2060. That changes how you think about almost everything: what a good quarter is, which customers matter, which shortcuts turn out to be expensive.

So this letter is not about this year. It is about the next ten. We will keep investing in people before machines, in service before sales, and in the unglamorous work of making things that last. We will report every quarter, as we must. But we will judge ourselves by the decade, and we ask you to do the same.

With thanks for your patience, which is the rarest capital of all,Hannah BeckerGroup chief executive · Hamburg, October 2026
