03 · Training captainWhat I teach new captains

Three things I say in every simulator.

  1. 01

    Fly the aircraft first.

    Whatever is happening, someone keeps flying. The checklist, the radio and the explanation come after.

  2. 02

    Say it early.

    The best first officers I have flown with spoke up while a problem was still small. I train captains to make that easy.

  3. 03

    Leave the ego at the gate.

    Four stripes on the sleeve do not make a decision right. The crew does, together, out loud.
