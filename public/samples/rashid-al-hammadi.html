Profile

A career measured the way pilots measure everything.

Hours, sectors and a clean safety record.

Rashid started as a flight instructor in 1999, flew the A330 and B777 as a first officer, and took his first long-haul command in 2012. Since 2017 he has trained and checked captains and first officers on line operations; more than 140 pilots have been released to the line under his supervision.

Crew noteKnown for calm decision-making in weather diversions, and for crew briefings that first officers still quote years later.
