01 · CasesThree routes, and the roads not taken

Every good strategy has a road it refused.

Client names are never published; these cases are illustrative. In each, the route in coral is what the board chose. The dotted line is the obvious option it turned down.

  1. Retail · 2023

    Too many stores, not enough customers.

    Not taken
    Defend every location.
    Chosen
    Close a third, and invest in the rest.
    Outcome
    Fewer stores, more customers in each.
  2. Banking · 2024

    An acquisition that looked cheap.

    Not taken
    Buy the competitor.
    Chosen
    Build the digital product instead.
    Outcome
    Kept the capital; launched in eighteen months.
  3. Manufacturing · 2025

    Going abroad, all at once.

    Not taken
    Enter five markets in a year.
    Chosen
    One market, done properly.
    Outcome
    Profitable abroad in the second year.
02 · MethodThree steps, always in this order

Map, mark, commit.

  1. i.

    Map the ground.

    Markets, costs, competitors, the board's appetite for risk. Before choosing, know the terrain you are standing on.

  2. ii.

    Mark where not to go.

    The hardest conversation, and the most useful one: the options the company will not pursue, written down and agreed.

  3. iii.

    Pick a route and commit.

    One route, with milestones the board can check. If the ground moves, re-plan the route, not the destination.
