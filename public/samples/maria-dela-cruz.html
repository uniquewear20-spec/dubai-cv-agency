FL 380Cruise · how I lead

Three habits I teach every new crew.

  1. i.

    Notice before they ask.

    The best service is the request nobody had to make: the blanket before the shiver, the glass of water before the headache.

  2. ii.

    Look after the crew first.

    A crew that feels backed up looks after the cabin. Clear briefings, fair breaks, and nobody left alone with a difficult moment.

  3. iii.

    Calm is contagious.

    Turbulence, a diversion, a medical call: guests read the crew's faces. I make sure what they read is calm.
