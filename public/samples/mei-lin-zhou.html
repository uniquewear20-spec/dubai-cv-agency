      About

      Slow practice, free playing.

      “I practise slowly so that on the night I can play freely. The audience should only ever hear the freedom.”

      Mei Lin Zhou

      Mei Lin grew up in Singapore, where she began the piano at five on an upright that had survived three families. She trained in Singapore and in Europe, and has since played as a soloist and chamber musician across Asia and Europe.

      Her own music grows out of the places she knows: the weight of monsoon air, harbour tides, lanterns in old streets. She teaches masterclasses for young pianists every season, and believes every student should compose something, however small.
