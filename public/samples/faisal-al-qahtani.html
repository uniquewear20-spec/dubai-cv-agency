Article III

Two languages, one meaning.

Saudi courts work in Arabic, so the Arabic text has to say exactly what the English says. Point at any phrase, or tab to it, to see its twin.

English

This Agreement is made in Arabic and in English. If the two texts differ, the Arabic text prevails.
