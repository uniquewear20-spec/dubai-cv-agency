Scene 1 · The story so far

Every frame a decision.

Karim Benjelloun writes, directs and shoots intimate films about cities at night. Three features, a documentary and a dozen short films, from Casablanca to Paris.

FADE IN:

Ext. Casablanca, the Corniche — dusk

A boy of ten films the Atlantic with his father's camcorder. He does not stop for an hour. The tape runs out before the light does.

Karim (V.O.)

I still frame everything as if the sea is about to change its mind.

Int. Paris, film school edit suite — night

Twenty-three years old. Three hours of rushes become eleven minutes of film. He cuts until the cleaners arrive.

Ext. High Atlas — dawn

A crew of forty waits for the light on the set of SABLE, his first feature. He waits longer than any of them.

Karim (V.O.)

Patience is the cheapest special effect there is.
