3The art of welcome
I.

Remember the name.

Recognition is the most personal luxury there is. Every colleague, from the doorman to the director of sales, learns the names of tonight's arrivals.

II.

Anticipate, never interrupt.

The best service is noticed only afterwards: the umbrella at the door before the rain, the quiet room ready before the meeting.

III.

Leave every room better than you found it.

Guests, colleagues, owners. I judge a year by what has improved, not by what has merely been maintained.
