Floor 1 · Profile

Service is a culture, not a checklist.

Layla leads luxury hotels the way a conductor leads an orchestra: every department hears the same tempo. She has opened four properties, repositioned two heritage hotels and built teams that guests ask for by name.

Her approach pairs warm, personal hospitality with commercial discipline. Revenue, owner relations and guest experience sit on the same page, reviewed every morning with the executive committee.

Born in Beirut and trained in Lausanne and Nice, she brings a Levantine sense of welcome to the Gulf's most demanding guests.

She mentors future general managers through an internal academy she founded in 2021, with eleven graduates now leading departments across the region.
