Signatures

Four drinks I'd put my name on.

  • Jamaica Paloma

    • 45 ml mezcal
    • 30 ml pink grapefruit
    • 15 ml hibiscus syrup
    • 10 ml lime
    • top: soda

    Hibiscus is jamaica back home. Chilli salt on half the rim, so you choose every sip.

  • Smoke & Tide

    • 50 ml mezcal
    • 15 ml dry sherry
    • 5 ml agave
    • 2 dashes bitters
    • pinch: sea salt

    Stirred, on one big cube. The salt makes the smoke taste like the coast at Puerto Escondido.

  • Desert Highball

    Zero-proof
    • 45 ml date and cardamom tea
    • 15 ml lemon
    • 10 ml honey
    • top: ginger beer

    Built for Dubai summers. Nobody misses the alcohol, and that is the point.

  • Sunday in Coyoacán

    Zero-proof
    • 60 ml hibiscus and cinnamon
    • 20 ml orange
    • 10 ml lime
    • dash: chilli tincture

    My grandmother's agua de jamaica, shaken hard and served up like a cocktail.
