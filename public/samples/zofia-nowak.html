      Small thingsWhat I teach every new waiter

      Service lives in the small things.

      1. i

        Be there at the second glance.

        A guest looks up twice before they ask for anything. The first glance is a thought; the second is a need.

      2. ii

        Never interrupt a story.

        A plate can wait three seconds. The end of somebody's story cannot.

      3. iii

        Remember the second visit.

        Names, favourite tables, allergies: written down after every service, so the next welcome is personal.
