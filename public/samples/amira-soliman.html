Clinical interests

Expert care for the whole heart.

Dr. Soliman works within a heart team of surgeons, imaging specialists and nurses, so every patient gets the right treatment, not just the one on offer.

Coronary intervention

Primary and elective angioplasty, including complex and chronic occlusion cases, with wrist (radial) access as standard.

Structural heart & TAVI

Transcatheter valve replacement for patients at high risk from open surgery, planned with the heart team.

Women's heart health

Heart disease often looks different in women. A dedicated clinic for diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation.

Prevention & risk

Risk assessment, cholesterol and blood-pressure care, and lifestyle programmes that keep patients out of the cath lab.

The patient journey

Clear steps, one doctor throughout.

Patients see the same consultant from the first appointment to the last follow-up.
