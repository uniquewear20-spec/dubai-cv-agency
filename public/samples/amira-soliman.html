For patients

Plain answers, before you ask.

The four reasons people most often come to see Dr Soliman, and what happens at the first appointment.

Chest pain and angina

Tightness when you walk uphill or climb stairs, easing when you rest.

First visit
Examination, ECG and, if needed, a CT scan of the heart arteries.

After a heart attack

Recovery, medicines and a safe return to work, driving and exercise.

First visit
A review of your treatment and a plan for the next twelve months.

Valve disease

Breathlessness or tiredness from a narrowed aortic valve, often in later life.

First visit
Echocardiogram and a discussion of keyhole valve replacement (TAVI).

Palpitations

A racing, fluttering or irregular heartbeat that comes and goes.

First visit
ECG and a small wearable monitor for a few days at home.
