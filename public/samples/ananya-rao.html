01 · Trace

One turn, nine exposures.

Kathak is built on the spin. In a chakkar the dancer turns on the heel, finds the audience again and lands exactly on the beat. My work slows that second down until you can see it.

1Exposure of 9
02 · WorksChoreography, 2019 – 2025

Four dances, drawn from above.

Before a piece is rehearsed, I draw its floor pattern: the path the body takes across the stage. These are the paths of four works.

2025 · Solo · 42 minutes

Chakkar

A solo built from a single turn, repeated and slowed until it becomes a landscape. The dancer spirals from the centre of the stage to its edges and back.

03 · LineageTraining, company, teaching

Twenty years on one floor.

Classical training first, then the rest of the world. Most of what I make still starts with the footwork I learned at seven.

  1. 2006

    First Kathak class

    Began training in Kathak in Bengaluru, at seven: footwork, rhythm and spins.

  2. 2016

    Contemporary dance

    Moved to London to train in contemporary technique, improvisation and choreography.

  3. 2018

    Company dancer

    Danced with touring companies across Europe for four seasons.

  4. 2019

    First solo

    Premiered The Weight of Silk and began making her own work.

  5. 2022

    Rao Movement

    Founded a small company for new work that joins Kathak and contemporary dance.
