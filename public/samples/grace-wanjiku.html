Leadership happens in small moments.

A typical day shift on Unit 4B, and the habits that keep 42 patients safe.

42beds68nurses3shifts
10:30now in Abu Dhabi
  1. 06:45

    Safety huddle

    Ten minutes with the night team: who is at risk of a fall, who is going home, which family needs a call.

  2. 07:30

    Bedside handover

    Handover happens at the bedside, with the patient, not at the nurses' station.

  3. 09:00

    Leadership rounds

    Grace sees every patient and asks the same question: is anything worrying you today?

  4. 12:30

    Teaching moment

    Fifteen minutes of practical teaching for new graduates and their preceptors.

  5. 15:00

    Family time

    An hour kept free for families with questions or concerns.

  6. 18:45

    Handover to nights

    A clear plan for the night, and a thank-you to the day team.
